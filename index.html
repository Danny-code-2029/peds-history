Focused Clinical Modules (Activate if Present)

1. Demographic Profile & Informant Reliability Tap to collapse/expand
Ask the family: "What is the child's full name, exact date of birth, and birth order? Who is taking care of the child daily, and where do you live?"
2. Immediate Red-Flag & IMNCI Emergency Triage Check first!
Ask urgently: "Is the child able to drink or breastfeed? Has the child been vomiting everything, having fits (convulsions), or seeming unusually sleepy or difficult to wake? Have they passed urine in the last 12 hours?"
3. Chief Complaints & History of Present Illness (HPI) Chronological order
Ask the informant: "What main problems brought you to the hospital today? Which symptom started first, and how many days ago was the child completely fine and playful?"
3A. Detailed Fever Characterization (The 8 Core Attributes & Exposures) Focused Fever Proforma
Ask about the fever: "Did the fever start suddenly or build up slowly? Did you measure it with a thermometer or just feel the forehead? Does it touch normal every day or stay high all day? Most importantly: when the fever comes down, is your child playing and feeding happily, or still dull and irritable?"
3B. Detailed Cough & Respiratory Characterization Focused Respiratory Proforma
Ask about the cough: "Does the cough sound dry or wet with phlegm? Does it come in continuous fits followed by a 'whoop' or vomiting? Is it worse at night or early morning? Do you hear any whistling sound (wheeze) from the chest?"
4. Systemic Review & Pertinent Negatives Rule out localizing signs
Ask systematically to rule out other foci: "Does the child have ear discharge, runny nose, loose stools, vomiting, crying while passing urine, joint swelling, or any bleeding/rash?"
5. Past Medical, Surgical & Allergy History Tap to expand
Ask the informant: "Has the child had similar complaints or nebulizations before? Any past hospital admissions, surgeries, blood transfusions, or known allergies to medicines or food?"
6. Antenatal, Natal & Postnatal (Neonatal) History Trimesters, Delivery & NICU
Ask the mother: "Did you have regular checkups and take Iron, Folic Acid, Calcium, and Tetanus shots during pregnancy? Did you have BP, sugar, or fever with rash? Was the baby born at full term in a hospital, and did the baby cry immediately after birth?"
7. Developmental History (4-Domain Assessment & DQ Calculator) Tap to expand
Ask the parents: "When did the child start holding their neck (3m), sitting alone (8m), walking (12-15m), speaking meaningful words (12m), and smiling socially (2m)? Has the child lost any skill they previously learned?"
8. Nutritional (24-Hr Recall) & Immunization History Breastfeeding, Weaning & Vaccines
Ask about feeding & vaccines: "Was colostrum given right after birth or was honey/ghutti given? Was the baby exclusively breastfed for 6 months? Any bottle feeding? What did the child eat yesterday from morning to night? Have all routine vaccines been given?"
9. Family, Socio-Economic (Kuppuswamy) & WASH Environment Consanguinity, TB Contact & Housing
Ask the family: "Were the parents related before marriage? Does anyone in the family have TB, chronic cough, asthma, or fits? What is the head of the family's education, job, and monthly income? Do you live in a pucca house, use LPG for cooking, and drink filtered/boiled water?"
