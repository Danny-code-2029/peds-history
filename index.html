          Clinical Pearl (Hand Regard & Midline Play — Fig 3.31 & 3.32): Hand regard is present even in children without vision; its persistence beyond 20 weeks is abnormal. At 3 to 4 months, the hands of the child come together in the midline as they play. Vision fixates on the mother's face at birth (8–10 inches away, up to 45° range, increasing to 90° by 4 weeks and 180° by 12 weeks).

              8. Nutritional History (Searchable 24h Recall, Table 7.6 & ICMR-NIN 2020 Standards)         Searchable Food Filter + Dynamic Quantity            
               
         
Core Nutritional Rules (Tables 7.1, 7.2 & Fig. 7.2):
         
• "1000-50-20" Energy Rule: Up to 10 kg = 1000 kcal; 10–20 kg = 1000 + 50 kcal/kg above 10 kg (e.g., 15 kg = 1250 kcal); >20 kg = 1500 + 20 kcal/kg above 20 kg (e.g., 30 kg = 1700 kcal).
         
• Macronutrient Yield (Table 7.1): Carbohydrate = 4 kcal/g (55–60% of diet) | Protein = 4 kcal/g (10–12%) | Fat = 9 kcal/g (25–30%) | Dietary Fiber = 2 kcal/g | Water = 0 kcal/g.
         
• NIN-ICMR "My Plate for the Day" (Fig. 7.2): Cereals 45% • Pulses 17% • Fats & Oils 12% • Milk & Curd 10% • Nuts & Seeds 8% • Vegetables 5% • Fruits 3%.

              15. Central Nervous System (CNS) Examination (SSIMS Protocol)         HMF, Cranial Nerves I–XII, Tone/MRC Power (0–5), Reflexes, Cerebellar & Meningeal            
       
          CNS Clinical Pearls (SSIMS Pages 8–12):           • Cranial Nerve Nuclei: Midbrain (III, IV) | Pons (V, VI, VII, VIII) | Medulla (IX, X, XI, XII). Extraocular muscles: SO4, LR6, Rest all CN III.
          • Spasticity vs. Rigidity: Spasticity (UMN pyramidal) is velocity-dependent, affects antigravity muscles (UL flexors, LL extensors) with clasp-knife phenomenon. Rigidity (Extrapyramidal/basal ganglia) is velocity-independent across agonists & antagonists equally (Lead-pipe or Cogwheel with tremor).
          • Deep Tendon Reflex Root Values: Jaw jerk (CN V / C5) • Biceps (C5, C6) • Supinator/Brachioradialis (C5, C6) • Triceps (C6, C7) • Knee/Patellar (L2, L3, L4) • Ankle/Achilles (S1, S2).

              16. Respiratory System Examination (SSIMS Protocol)         Upper/Lower Tract, TVF, Percussion & Adventitious Breath Sounds            
       
          Respiratory Clinical Pearls (SSIMS Pages 13–15):           • Tactile Vocal Fremitus (TVF): Increased in Consolidation (Lobar pneumonia), TB cavity. Decreased in Pleural effusion, Pneumothorax, Fibrosis.
          • Percussion Notes: Stony dull = Pleural effusion | Woody/Impaired dull = Consolidation, Collapse | Hyperresonant = Pneumothorax, Emphysema.
          • Adventitious Sounds: Fine crackles (late inspiration — interstitial/early edema); Coarse crackles (early inspiration — bronchiectasis/pneumonia); Wheeze (high-pitched musical, expiration — asthma); Rhonchi (low-pitched snoring, clears with cough — secretions); Stridor (harsh inspiratory — croup/foreign body); Pleural rub (leathery grating in both phases).        
       
         
                                 
         
                                 
         
                                 
         
                                 
       
     
   
       
              17. Cardiovascular System (CVS) Examination (SSIMS Protocol)         Precordium, Apex Beat, Parasternal Heave, Thrills & 6 Auscultatory Areas            
       
          CVS Auscultation Areas (SSIMS Pages 16–18 — "All People Enjoy Time Magazine"):           • Mitral Area (5th Left ICS medial to MCL): MDM (low-pitched rough rumbling with bell in left lateral position on expiration); PSM/HSM (high-pitched soft blowing with diaphragm radiating to axilla/back).
          • Tricuspid Area (4th Left ICS lower sternal border): Murmurs accentuate on inspiration (Carvallo's sign).
          • Pulmonary Area (2nd Left ICS) & Aortic Area (2nd Right ICS): Crescendo-decrescendo Ejection Systolic Murmur (ESM — aortic radiates to carotids) & Early Diastolic Murmur (EDM — sitting leaning forward).
          • Erb's Area (3rd Left ICS) & Gibson's Area (Left 1st ICS infraclavicular): Continuous machinery murmur peaking around S2 (PDA).
