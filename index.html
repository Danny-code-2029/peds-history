Activate Focused Clinical Modules (As Needed)

1. Demographic Profile & Informant Reliability Tap to collapse/expand
Ask the informant: "What is the child's full name, exact age (or date of birth), and birth order? Who is the primary caregiver giving this history today, and where is your current residence and native place?"
2. Clinical Red-Flags Screen Every Sick Child
Ask urgently: "Is the child able to drink or breastfeed? Does the child vomit everything they take? Have they had any fits (convulsions) during this illness, or are they unusually sleepy/difficult to wake? Have they passed urine in the last 12 hours?"
3. Chief Complaints & History of Present Illness (HPI) Chronological order in patient's words
Ask the informant: "What are the main complaints that brought you here today, and how many days has each been present? When was the child last completely well and playful? Did the illness start suddenly or gradually?"
3A. Detailed Fever Proforma (The 8 Core Attributes & Tropical Exposures) Active Module
Ask the 8 Fever Attributes: "Was the temperature measured with a thermometer? Does it touch normal baseline every day (intermittent) or stay elevated all day (continuous/remittent)? Are there chills/rigors or night sweats? Crucially: between fever spikes when the temperature drops, is the child active and playing, or still dull and irritable?"
3B. Detailed Cough & Respiratory Proforma Active Module
Ask about Cough & Breathing: "Did the cough start suddenly while eating/playing (foreign body) or gradually? Is it dry, wet, barking like a seal, or coming in fits with a 'whoop' and vomiting? Is it worse at night? Is there any whistling sound (wheeze), harsh inspiratory noise (stridor), or cough while drinking milk?"
3C. Hydration Status Active Module
Ask for Fluid Losses & Hydration Clues: "How many watery stools or vomiting episodes has the child had? Is the child drinking eagerly/thirsty, or too weak to drink? Are the eyes sunken, tears absent when crying, mouth dry, and urine output reduced?"
4. Systemic Review & Pertinent Negatives Rule out other organ foci
Ask systematically: "Does the child have headache, fits, ear pain/discharge, sore throat, vomiting, loose motions, crying while passing urine, red/cola-colored urine, puffiness around the eyes, suck-rest-suck cycle while feeding, joint swelling, or bleeding spots?"
5. Past Medical, Surgical, Allergen & Addiction History Tap to expand
Ask about Past History: "Has the child had similar illnesses, hospital admissions, prior surgeries, or known drug/food allergies? For adolescents or older children, inquire about tobacco, alcohol, or substance habits."
6. Antenatal, Birth & Neonatal History Tap to expand
Ask the mother: "During pregnancy, did you take regular supplements and checkups? Was there any high BP, bleeding, or infection? Did the baby cry right after birth, and what was the birth weight? Did the baby pass meconium and urine normally?"
7. Growth, Anthropometry & Developmental History Includes MPH, Fluids & DQ Calculators
Clinical Growth & Development Reference: Birth weight (3 kg) triples by 1 yr (9 kg) and quadruples by 2 yr (12 kg). Head circumference: 34 cm at birth, 46 cm at 1 yr. Milestones: Neck holding at 3 mo, sitting alone at 8 mo, walking alone at 15 mo, meaningful words at 12 mo.
Ask the parents: "Has the child been gaining weight and height normally? When did milestones like neck holding, sitting, and walking occur? What is the daily screen time or any behavioral habits?"
8. Nutritional History & Immunization Auto Calorie & Protein Deficit
Ask about Feeding & Immunization: "Was breastfeeding started within 1 hour and exclusively for 6 months? When was complementary feeding started? What did the child eat yesterday? Are vaccinations up to date?"
8B. Adolescent Health & HEADSS Inquiry (Ages 10–19 yr) Active Module
Adolescent Checklist: Assess academics, sleep, diet, pubertal development (SMR), emotional well-being, peer relations, and confidential health habits.
9. Family History, Socio-Economic Class & WASH Environment Tap to expand
Ask the family: "Are parents related by blood? Any family history of TB, asthma, or genetic disorders? What is the family income, housing, and drinking water source?"
