ℹ️ About Pediatric History Performa

Clinical Case-Taking & Bedside Decision Support System

Pediatric History Performa is an interactive bedside clinical history-taking web application designed for medical students, interns, and pediatric residents to systematically record, calculate, and synthesize pediatric case presentations.

Key Clinical & Technical Capabilities:
  • Structured 13-Section Proforma: Covers Demographics, Emergency Triage, Chief Complaints Timeline, Present Illness, Past/Surgical History, Birth & Preterm Corrected Age, Growth & Holliday-Segar Fluids, Developmental Quotient (DQ), Nutritional Deficit, Interactive NIS Immunization Checklist, Allergens, Addictions, 3-Generation SVG Pedigree Builder, and GPE/Vitals.
  • 2-Column Google Sheet Case Registry: Automatically generates shareable patient case hyperlinks paired with chief complaints for seamless ward logbook archiving.
  • Personal Firebase Cloud AI Vault: Links each clinician's personal Gemini API key to their Google account during active sessions and wipes the session key upon logout.
