Activate Focused Clinical Modules (As Needed)

1. Demographic Profile & Informant Reliability Tap to collapse/expand
Ask the informant: "What is the child's full name, exact age (or date of birth), and birth order? Who is the primary caregiver giving this history today, and where is your current residence and native place?"
2. Immediate IMNCI & Emergency Red-Flag Triage Screen Every Sick Child
Ask urgently: "Is the child able to drink or breastfeed? Does the child vomit everything they take? Have they had any fits (convulsions) during this illness, or are they unusually sleepy/difficult to wake? Have they passed urine in the last 12 hours?"
3. Chief Complaints & History of Present Illness (HPI) Chronological order in patient's words
Ask the informant: "What are the main complaints that brought you here today, and how many days has each been present? When was the child last completely well and playful? Did the illness start suddenly or gradually?" (Record raw symptoms, not self-reported diagnoses like 'typhoid').
3A. Detailed Fever Proforma (The 8 Core Attributes & Tropical Exposures) Active Module
Ask the 8 Fever Attributes: "Was the temperature measured with a thermometer? Does it touch normal baseline every day (intermittent) or stay elevated all day (continuous/remittent)? Are there chills/rigors or night sweats? Crucially: between fever spikes when the temperature drops, is the child active and playing, or still dull and irritable?"
3B. Detailed Cough & Respiratory Proforma Active Module
Ask about Cough & Breathing: "Did the cough start suddenly while eating/playing (foreign body) or gradually? Is it dry, wet, barking like a seal, or coming in fits with a 'whoop' and vomiting? Is it worse at night? Is there any whistling sound (wheeze), harsh inspiratory noise (stridor), or cough while drinking milk?"
3C. Dehydration Assessment & Holliday-Segar Fluid Calculator (Ghai Ch. 5) Active Module
Ask for Fluid Losses & Hydration Clues (Table 5.3): "How many watery stools or vomiting episodes has the child had? Is the child drinking eagerly/thirsty, or too weak to drink? Are the eyes sunken, tears absent when crying, mouth dry, and urine output reduced?"
4. Head-to-Toe Systemic Review & Pertinent Negatives Rule out other organ foci
Ask systematically: "Does the child have headache, fits, ear pain/discharge, sore throat, vomiting, loose motions, crying while passing urine, red/cola-colored urine, puffiness around the eyes, suck-rest-suck cycle while feeding, joint swelling, or bleeding spots?"
5. Past Medical, Antenatal (Trimesters), Natal & Neonatal History Tap to expand
Ask the mother: "Has the child had similar illnesses, recurrent wheezing/diarrhea, hospital admissions, or blood transfusions before? During pregnancy, did you take Folate in the 1st trimester, Iron/Calcium/Tetanus shots in the 2nd, and have regular checkups? Was there any fever with rash (TORCH), high BP, diabetes, or leaking water >18 hours? Did the baby cry right after birth, and what was the birth weight?"
6. Physical Growth, Dentition & Short Stature Inquiry (Ghai Ch. 2) Includes MPH & Fluid Calculators
Ghai Ch. 2 Benchmarks: Birth wt ($3\text{ kg}$) doubles by $5\text{ mo}$ ($6\text{ kg}$), triples by $1\text{ yr}$ ($9\text{ kg}$), quadruples by $2\text{ yr}$ ($12\text{ kg}$); $3\text{ yr}=15\text{ kg}$, $4\text{ yr}=16\text{ kg}$. Length: Birth $50\text{ cm}$, $6\text{ mo}$ $65\text{ cm}$, $1\text{ yr}$ $75\text{ cm}$, $2\text{ yr}$ $90\text{ cm}$, $4\text{ yr}$ $100\text{ cm}$. HC: Birth $34\text{ cm}$, $1\text{ yr}$ $46\text{ cm}$ (chest = head at $1\text{ yr}$). Primary lower central incisors erupt at $6\text{–}10\text{ mo}$.
Ask the parents (Table 2.14 Short Stature & FTT Clues): "Has the child been gaining weight and height like peers? When did the first milk teeth erupt? Does the child have excessive urination (polyuria), bulky/greasy stools, chronic constipation/lethargy, or did either parent have a late growth spurt in school?"
7. 5-Domain Developmental, Red Flags & Behavioral History (Ghai Ch. 3) Includes DQ & Behavioral Screen
Ghai Ch. 3 Key Milestones & WHO Red-Flag Cutoffs: Gross Motor: Neck holding ($3\text{m}$), Rolls over ($5\text{m}$), Tripod sit ($6\text{m}$), Sits alone ($8\text{m}$; Red flag $>10\text{m}$), Stands with support ($9\text{m}$), Walks alone ($15\text{m}$; Red flag $>18\text{m}$), Tricycle ($3\text{y}$). Fine Motor: Bidextrous reach ($4\text{m}$), Unidextrous transfer ($6\text{m}$), Immature pincer ($9\text{m}$), Mature pincer ($12\text{m}$), Scribbles/3-block tower ($18\text{m}$), Circle ($3\text{y}$), Cross ($4\text{y}$). Social: Social smile ($2\text{m}$), Stranger anxiety ($6\text{m}$), Waves bye ($9\text{m}$), Domestic mimicry ($18\text{m}$). Language: Coos ($3\text{m}$), Monosyllables ($6\text{m}$), Bisyllables ($9\text{m}$), 1–2 meaningful words ($12\text{m}$; Red flag $>18\text{m}$). Vision/Hearing: Fixates face ($1\text{m}$; Red flag $>2\text{m}$), Turns head to sound ($3\text{–}4\text{m}$), Diagonal localization ($10\text{m}$).
Ask across all 5 Domains & Habits: "When did the child hold their neck, sit alone, walk, use a pincer grasp, smile socially, and speak meaningful words? Does the child see and turn to sound normally? Has the child ever lost a skill they previously learned? Also ask: How many hours of TV/mobile screen does the child watch daily (>1–2 hr delays speech)? Do they eat mud/plaster/chalk (pica), hold their breath until blue/pale when crying (breath-holding spells), or have tantrums/hyperactivity?"
8. Nutritional History (Ghai Ch. 6 ICMR RDA Calculator) & Immunization Auto Calorie & Protein Deficit
Ask about Feeding & 24-Hr Recall: "Was breastfeeding started within 1 hour of birth and colostrum fed? Were any pre-lacteal feeds (honey/ghutti/sugar water) given? Was the baby exclusively breastfed for 6 months, or given diluted cow/formula milk in a bottle? When did weaning start, and is oil/ghee added? Tell me everything the child ate and drank yesterday in household measures (katori/glass/tsp)."
8B. Adolescent Health Visit & HEADSS / SMR Inquiry (Ghai Ch. 4, Ages 10–19 yr) Active Module
Ghai Table 4.4 Checklist: First ask with parents: academic performance, school absenteeism, calcium/iron/dairy intake, junk food, sleep duration, and menarche (avg 12.6 yr, 2–2.5 yr after breast buds). Then interview the adolescent confidentially (with consent): body image, mood/anxiety, peer relationships, tobacco/alcohol/substance use, reproductive health concerns, and Td (10, 16 yr) / HPV vaccines.
9. Family Pedigree, Socio-Economic Class & WASH Environment Tap to expand
Ask the family: "Are the parents related by blood (consanguinity)? How many members live in the household, and are all siblings healthy? Does anyone at home or in the neighborhood have TB, chronic cough, asthma, epilepsy, or thalassemia? What is the head of the family's education, occupation, and monthly income? Do you use LPG or wood/biomass chulha, drink filtered/boiled water, and have a sanitary toilet?"
